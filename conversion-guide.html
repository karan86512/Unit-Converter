What is Unit Conversion?

Unit conversion is the process of changing a measurement from one unit to another while keeping the same quantity.

For example, a distance can be expressed in kilometers, miles, meters or feet. The value changes, but the actual distance stays the same.

Example 1 kilometer = 0.621371 miles
02

Length Conversion

Length measures the distance between two points. Common units include meters, kilometers, centimeters, inches, feet, yards and miles.

Temperature Conversion

The most common temperature scales are Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

Fahrenheit → Celsius (°F − 32) × 5/9
