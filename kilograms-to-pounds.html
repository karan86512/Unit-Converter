Kilograms to Pounds: Formula, Table & Examples

Learn how to convert kilograms to pounds and pounds to kilograms using simple formulas, practical examples and an easy conversion table.

📖 8 min read • Updated July 2026

Kilograms and pounds are two of the most common units used to describe weight or mass. Kilograms are widely used in the Metric System, while pounds are commonly used in the United States and in some other contexts.

If you have ever seen a weight in kilograms and needed to understand it in pounds, a simple conversion formula can help.

What Is a Kilogram?

A kilogram, abbreviated as kg, is the base unit of mass in the International System of Units (SI).

Kilograms are commonly used around the world for measuring body weight, luggage, food, products, industrial materials and many other quantities.

Example

A person's body weight might be 60 kg.

What Is a Pound?

A pound, commonly abbreviated as lb, is a unit of mass or weight used mainly in the United States and some other countries.

Pounds are frequently used for body weight, food quantities, shipping weights and everyday measurements.

Example

A person's weight might be 150 lb.

Kilograms to Pounds Formula

To convert kilograms to pounds, multiply the kilogram value by approximately 2.20462.

Kilograms → Pounds lb = kg × 2.20462

For many everyday calculations, you can use 2.2 as a convenient approximation.

Quick Approximation 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb

Example: Convert 1 Kilogram to Pounds

Let's convert 1 kg into pounds.

lb = 1 × 2.20462
lb = 2.20462
Answer

1 kg ≈ 2.20462 lb

Pounds to Kilograms Formula

Converting pounds to kilograms works in the opposite direction.

Divide the pound value by approximately 2.20462.

Pounds → Kilograms kg = lb ÷ 2.20462

Another commonly used formula is:

Alternative Formula kg = lb × 0.45359237

Example: Convert 100 Pounds to Kilograms

Let's convert 100 pounds into kilograms.

kg = 100 ÷ 2.20462
kg ≈ 45.3592
Answer

100 lb ≈ 45.36 kg

Where Are Kilograms and Pounds Used?

Both units appear frequently in everyday life, but their usage depends on the country and industry.

  • Body weight: kilograms or pounds depending on the country.
  • Food: kilograms and grams are common in Metric countries.
  • Shipping: both kilograms and pounds may be used.
  • Travel: luggage limits may be shown in kilograms or pounds.
  • Fitness: gym equipment may use kilograms or pounds.

Common Kilogram to Pound Conversion Mistakes

A few mistakes can lead to incorrect results when converting between kilograms and pounds.

  • Using 2.20462 in the wrong direction.
  • Confusing pounds with ounces.
  • Rounding too early.
  • Forgetting which unit is the starting value.
  • Mixing up kg and lb labels.

Easy Mental Conversion Method

For a quick estimate, you can remember that 1 kilogram is approximately 2.2 pounds.

Quick Estimate Pounds ≈ Kilograms × 2.2

For example, if you have 30 kg:

30 × 2.2 = 66 lb

The exact value is approximately 66.14 pounds, so the mental calculation gives a useful quick estimate.
