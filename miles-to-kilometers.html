Miles to Kilometers: Formula, Table & Examples

Learn how to convert miles to kilometers and kilometers to miles using simple formulas, practical examples and an easy conversion table.

📖 8 min read • Updated July 2026

Miles and kilometers are two common units used to measure distance. Kilometers are part of the Metric System and are used in most countries, while miles are commonly used for road distances in the United States and a few other places.

If you are travelling, reading a map, checking a running distance or comparing road distances, knowing how to convert miles to kilometers can be very useful.

What Is a Mile?

A mile, abbreviated as mi, is a unit of distance commonly used in the United States and some other countries.

Miles are frequently used for road distances, speed limits, running distances and travel measurements.

Important Conversion

1 international mile = 1.609344 kilometers

What Is a Kilometer?

A kilometer, abbreviated as km, is a unit of distance in the Metric System.

One kilometer is equal to 1,000 meters.

Important Conversion

1 kilometer = 1,000 meters

Miles to Kilometers Formula

To convert miles to kilometers, multiply the number of miles by 1.609344.

Miles → Kilometers km = miles × 1.609344

For quick mental calculations, you can use approximately 1.6 kilometers for every mile.

Quick Approximation 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km

Example: Convert 1 Mile to Kilometers

Let's convert 1 mile to kilometers.

km = 1 × 1.609344
km = 1.609344
Answer

1 mile = 1.609344 km

Example: Convert 5 Miles to Kilometers

km = 5 × 1.609344
km = 8.04672
Answer

5 miles ≈ 8.05 km

Example: Convert 10 Miles to Kilometers

km = 10 × 1.609344
km = 16.09344
Answer

10 miles ≈ 16.09 km

Kilometers to Miles Formula

To convert kilometers to miles, divide the kilometer value by 1.609344.

Kilometers → Miles miles = km ÷ 1.609344

You can also multiply kilometers by approximately 0.621371.

Alternative Formula miles = km × 0.621371

Example: Convert 100 Kilometers to Miles

Let's convert 100 kilometers into miles.

miles = 100 ÷ 1.609344
miles ≈ 62.1371
Answer

100 km ≈ 62.14 miles

Where Are Miles and Kilometers Used?

The unit used for distance depends largely on the country and the measurement system it follows.

  • United States: Miles are commonly used for road distances and speed limits.
  • Most of Europe: Kilometers are generally used for road distances.
  • Asia: Kilometers are commonly used for roads, travel and everyday measurements.
  • Running: Both miles and kilometers are used depending on the country and event.
  • Travel: International travellers often need to convert between the two units.

Miles to Kilometers for Travel

Distance conversion is particularly useful when travelling between countries that use different measurement systems.

For example, if a road sign or navigation system shows a destination as 50 miles away, you can estimate the distance in kilometers by multiplying by 1.6.

50 × 1.6 ≈ 80 km

The exact value is 80.4672 kilometers.

Miles and Kilometers for Running

Common Miles to Kilometers Conversion Mistakes

Distance conversions are simple, but some common mistakes can produce inaccurate results.

  • Confusing miles with nautical miles.
  • Using 1.6 when an exact calculation is required.
  • Dividing instead of multiplying when converting miles to kilometers.
  • Rounding the result too early.
  • Confusing miles per hour with kilometers per hour.

Easy Mental Conversion Method

For quick estimates, remember this simple rule:

Quick Estimate 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km

For example, if you need to convert 25 miles:

25 × 1.6 = 40 km

The exact answer is 40.2336 km, so the mental method gives a very close estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many kilometers are in 1 mile?

One international mile is exactly 1.609344 kilometers.

How many miles are in 1 kilometer?

One kilometer is approximately 0.621371 miles.

How many kilometers are 10 miles?

10 miles are approximately 16.09344 kilometers, or 16.09 km rounded to two decimal places.

How many miles are 100 kilometers?

100 kilometers are approximately 62.1371 miles, or 62.14 miles rounded to two decimal places.

Is 5 miles close to 8 kilometers?

Yes. 5 miles is approximately 8.04672 kilometers, so it is commonly rounded to about 8 kilometers.

Conclusion

Miles and kilometers are both useful units for measuring distance. The exact relationship between them is easy to remember:

Key Conversion 1 mile = 1.609344 km

To convert miles to kilometers, multiply the number of miles by 1.609344. To convert kilometers to miles, divide by 1.609344 or multiply by approximately 0.621371.

For quick estimates, remembering that one mile is approximately 1.6 kilometers is usually enough.
